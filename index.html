Methods and data sources

Mass fractions are combined by the mixture rule. Density defaults to an ideal additive-volume estimate, assuming no porosity; replace it with a measured value. Transmission is exp(-μ/ρ × density × thickness), for a uniform sheet at normal incidence. Lead equivalence is energy-specific.

Flexibility, toxicity, manufacturing feasibility and dose are not predicted. NIST mass attenuation tables: polyethylene, tungsten, bismuth, lead.
